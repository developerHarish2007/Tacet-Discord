      About TACET DISCORD

      TACET DISCORD is an AI-powered Factory Incident Intelligence System designed to help technicians detect, investigate, verify, and resolve machine failures safely. It bridges the gap between multimodal AI, predictive maintenance, industrial memory, and active evidence investigation.

      Core Intelligence Capabilities

      Five specialized pillars of industrial plant diagnosis and verification

      Visual Inspection

      Detects and localizes visible surface anomalies with ResNet-18 spatial heatmaps, 20-pass MC Dropout uncertainty, and OCR.

      Predictive Maintenance

      Analyzes multi-channel telemetry (RMS, Kurtosis) and estimates machine degradation, Remaining Useful Life (RUL), and SHAP attribution.

      Machine Memory

      Recalls previous incidents and preserves senior technician tribal knowledge via 384-d semantic dense vector search in SQLite.

      VEIL — Active Evidence Intelligence

      VEIL helps TACET determine not only what the evidence suggests, but what evidence should be investigated next. Features evidence sufficiency measurement, hypothesis ranking, next-best-evidence selection, contradiction hunting, investigation replay, novel failure exploration, and evidence-grounded re-evaluation.

      Verification Gate

      Cross-checks conclusions against deterministic safety rules, detects conflicts, and blocks unsupported or hallucinated claims.

      How TACET Thinks (VEIL Active Inquiry) The Key Idea

      TACET doesn't blindly guess when evidence is missing. If confidence or evidence is low, the VEIL Active Inquiry Engine determines what evidence to acquire next, ranks the best action, and re-evaluates the incident once new sensor readings or photos arrive.

      Trust & Safety Gate

      TACET enforces strict 3-tier deterministic confidence:

      VERIFIED (Tier 1)

      Strong evidence & high consensus. Auto-surfaces confirmed diagnosis and fix.

      TENTATIVE (Tier 2)

      Conflicting or partial evidence. Flags uncertainty and specialist to consult.

      ESCALATE (Tier 3)

      Insufficient evidence / ungrounded. Safety warning & mandatory senior review.
